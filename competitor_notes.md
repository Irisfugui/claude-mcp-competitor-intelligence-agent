# Competitor Notes

## BOYLE Sports
### Homepage
- Strong emphasis on offers and immediate action
- Promotion-led messaging
- Clear CTA language like “Join”, “Bet”, “Play”
- Messaging feels direct and conversion-focused

### Brand Impression
- Strong hybrid retail and online presence
- More transactional than community-led
- Good visibility for offers and product-led messaging

---

## Paddy Power
### Homepage / Games
- Bold and high-energy brand style
- Clear focus on gaming, offers, and entertainment
- Strong brand identity and aggressive CTA approach

### Brand Impression
- Very strong consumer-facing messaging
- Memorable tone of voice
- Good at combining entertainment and conversion

---

## QuinnBet
### Homepage
- Simpler, more digital-first layout
- Focus on online betting and casino
- Messaging feels more straightforward and less brand-heavy

### Brand Impression
- Online-first competitor
- Less community feel
- More focused on direct digital engagement
