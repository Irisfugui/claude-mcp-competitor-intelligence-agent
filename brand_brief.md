# Harbour Play

## Business Overview
Harbour Play is an Ireland-based gaming and entertainment brand with a hybrid business model:
- 9 physical venues across Ireland
- 1 online gaming platform
- a loyalty and membership programme

The business wants to improve how it uses SEO, competitor intelligence, and marketing planning to drive repeat visits and online engagement.

## Audience
Primary:
- Adults 25–45 in Ireland
- Interested in casual gaming, entertainment, promotions, and loyalty rewards

Secondary:
- Inactive customers
- Repeat customers
- Customers near venue locations

## Brand Personality
- Friendly
- Energetic
- Clear
- Modern
- Responsible

## Tone of Voice
- Straightforward
- Local and approachable
- Promotional but not pushy

## Main Business Goals
1. Increase repeat visits to physical venues
2. Improve online customer retention
3. Grow loyalty programme engagement
4. Improve local search visibility
5. Create more consistent campaign planning

## Agent Objective
The agent should:
- analyze real competitor websites
- identify SEO and content patterns
- compare messaging and CTA styles
- spot content gaps and keyword opportunities
- recommend next-step marketing actions
